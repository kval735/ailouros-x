Ailouros is the ancient Greek word for cat. X carries two meanings here, it can mean gone or dead, and it is also the Roman numeral for 10. A cat has 9 lives, so the 10th would be its last. Put it together: the dead cat. It all ties back to one idea: curiosity killed the cat. This site exists for the purpose of sharing your curious thoughts.

As people moved away, these curious conversations got harder to have. Ailouros X is the attempt to keep them going — a little corner of the internet that holds that same energy, wherever everyone ends up.

Inspired by Isla, Ruby, and Olli.

How to use it

🌀 Ponder

Short, sharp curiosity. Shower thoughts, weird questions, things that randomly crossed your mind. No pressure to have an answer — the point is the thought itself. Keep it brief, keep it curious.

📖 Deep Dive

A bit more developed. Write a short essay on a concept you've been thinking about, something you recently learned, a topic you went down a rabbit hole on. Think of it as a summary of your brain lately.

🖊️ The Wall

Leave your mark. Draw something, scribble something, write your name. It's a shared canvas. The wall remembers everyone who's been here.

💬 The Chat

Less curiosity, more chaos. Message whatever the fuck you want.
